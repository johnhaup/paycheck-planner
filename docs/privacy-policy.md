# Paycheck Planner Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Paycheck Planner does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## Data you enter

Income sources, expenses, and settings you enter are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and does not use third-party services that collect data.

## Payment links

If you add a payment link to an expense, tapping it opens that website in your browser. That website's own privacy policy applies to anything you do there.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
