# HAU-417: clearing a bill's due day brings back its old schedule (2026-11-16)

## What changed
- `app/expense-form.tsx`: saving with a due day no longer wipes `linkSchedule` / `paycheckOrdinal`.
  The schedule is saved whenever the source has more than one paycheck a month (the due day
  already wins in `isExpenseLinkedToPaycheck`, `isOncePerMonth` and the list meta), so deleting
  the due day later shows and saves the Monthly / 2nd choice the bill had before.
- `linkSchedule` is now also stored as `"every-paycheck"` (not undefined) for every-paycheck bills
  on multi-paycheck sources. Every reader treats anything but `"monthly"` as every paycheck.
- A bill saved with a due day before this fix has no schedule left. Its form now defaults How
  Often to Monthly (1st paycheck) instead of Every paycheck, so clearing the day keeps it
  once a month rather than silently multiplying its cost.
- "Which Paycheck" stays hidden while a due day is typed (it was keyed on `isMonthly`, which no
  longer excludes the due day).

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint` clean.
- `Paycheck Planner Dev`, Release build, sample data, scratch Maestro flow (not committed): the
  issue's steps. Internet → due 18 (How Often hidden) → list "Due the 18th · monthly" → clear the
  18 → form shows How Often **Monthly** and Which Paycheck → save → "2nd paycheck each month ·
  monthly", Brightline back to $1,477.33/mo (was $1,559.00 with the bug).
- Not run on the simulator: the Monthly default for a bill saved with a due day before this fix
  (needs pre-fix data); it is the `useState` initialiser only.

## For QA
- No Maestro flow changes needed.

## Pitfall
- `isMonthly` in the form now means "the chosen schedule is monthly", due day or not; gate UI
  on `!hasDueDay` separately.
