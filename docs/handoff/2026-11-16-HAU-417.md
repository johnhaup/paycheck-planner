# HAU-417: clearing a bill's due day brings back its old schedule (2026-11-16)

## What changed
- `app/expense-form.tsx`: saving with a due day keeps the user's own How Often choice
  (`savedSchedule`) and Which Paycheck slot instead of wiping them. While the day is set it still
  decides the paycheck, so nothing changes until it is cleared; then the form shows and saves the
  choice the bill had before (Internet: Monthly / 2nd, not Every paycheck).
- Under a due day "every-paycheck" is written out, not left undefined: a due day with no schedule
  is a bill saved before this fix, and its form defaults How Often to Monthly (1st), so clearing
  the day keeps it once a month rather than silently multiplying its cost.
- Merge with HAU-350 (repeat schedules, 2026-11-16): main's derived `schedule` (a due day forces
  a by-month schedule) still drives the chips, First Month and placement; only what is *saved*
  differs. `repeatStart` is kept under a due day too. Every other paycheck can now be saved next
  to a due day, so `describeLinkSchedule` and `cadenceLabel` (`src/utils/expenseSections.ts`)
  ignore it while a due day is set; payDates readers already did (`getExpenseRepeatMonths`).
- `.maestro/qa/expenses.yaml`: QA's clear-the-due-day steps (Internet back to "2nd paycheck each
  month", Brightline $1,477.33/mo) sit before HAU-350's QA Water yearly steps.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint` clean after the merge.
- Scratch `bun test` (not committed), bi-weekly source: due 18 with monthly/2nd, every paycheck,
  every other paycheck, or no schedule (old shape) all read "Due the 18th · monthly", $70/mo, land
  Dec 18 / Jan 15 / Feb 12; without the day each goes back to its own schedule.
- Release app (QA 27's `.app` copy, merged JS bundle swapped in, re-signed) on a throwaway iOS 27
  simulator: `smoke.yaml` + `expenses.yaml` 2/2. Scratch flow: Groceries → Every other paycheck →
  due 5 ("Due the 5th · monthly", Brightline $1,220.67/mo) → clear → Every other paycheck again.
- Before the merge: the issue's steps on a Release build, and QA's run (legacy data → Monthly / 1st).

## For QA
- Expenses → Internet → due day 18 → save → clear → How Often Monthly, Which Paycheck 2nd.
- New with HAU-350: a bill on Every other paycheck → type a due day (chips offer only month
  schedules) → save → reopen and clear → How Often comes back on Every other paycheck.

## Pitfalls
- `Paycheck Planner Dev` is shared: HAU-421's `expo run:ios` reinstalled it mid-flow (app went to
  the home screen). For a bundle swap, `simctl create` a scratch simulator and delete it after.
- Under a due day the saved `linkSchedule` can be a paycheck schedule; readers must check `dueDay`
  first (or go through `getExpenseRepeatMonths`), never `linkSchedule` alone.
