# HAU-619: outline buttons visible in both modes

## What changed and why
`AppButton`'s outline variant drew a hairline in `colors.border`. In Light that is `lightGray`
#E5E5EA, about 1.1:1 on the `offWhite` screen, so "Try with sample data", "Add one-time expense",
"Restore purchases" and the amount sheet's reset button read as bold text, not buttons. In Dark,
`slate650` on `slate950` was about 1.4:1.

- New palette colour `buttonOutline`: Light `systemGray` #8E8E93 (2.9:1 on offWhite, 3.3:1 on
  the white modal), Dark `slate550` #6B7380 (3.8:1 on the screen, 3.5:1 on the modal).
- The outline is 1pt instead of a hairline.
- `border` itself is unchanged: inputs, chips, the date picker, toasts, the goal track and
  PaidProgress use it as before.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint` clean.
- Scratch Maestro flows on Runner 2 (dev build on Metro), screenshots read: Welcome (the empty
  state), the bottom of Pay date, and the paywall footer, each in Dark and Light. All three
  outlines are clearly visible on the screen and the modal backgrounds.

## Pitfalls
- Do not darken `border` itself for this: it is also a fill (goal track, PaidProgress) and the
  input outline. That would change those components too (see HAU-536).
