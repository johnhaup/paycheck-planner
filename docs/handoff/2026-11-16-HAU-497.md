# HAU-497 Flaky gate flow: smoke, expenses (2026-11-16)

## What failed (gate @ 8dc033c, artifacts in `~/runner/builds/paycheck-planner/2026-11-16-210125`)
Both flows hit the same ~18 s host stall (21:15:58 to 21:16:17, four shards running):
- **expenses**: `scrollUntilVisible "Yearly"` with `centerElement: true`. "Yearly" was on screen,
  100% visible, at the end of the form, where it can never reach the centre. Maestro kept swiping
  at it (bounds never changed) and one lookup took 18 s, so the 20 s budget ran out. The step
  before it ("Mesa Health") had already burned 11 s the same way.
- **smoke**: `stopApp` then `launchApp`; the launch failed after 10 s with `Simulator device failed
  to launch … did not return a process handle nor launch error` and left the home screen.

## Change (`.maestro/qa/smoke.yaml`, `.maestro/qa/expenses.yaml` only; the app is untouched)
- expenses: no `scrollUntilVisible` left (there were 10). Each is now explicit swipes to the known
  end (list top for Internet, list end for QA Water, form end for everything in the form) and an
  `extendedWaitUntil` of 15 s on the element. Form swipes are short and in the upper half
  (45% to 15%), so they also work with the keyboard up.
- The form grows as it is filled in (linking a source adds due day and How Often, clearing the
  due day adds Which Paycheck, Yearly adds First Month), so there are swipes after each of those
  before the wait for the button. `assertVisible: "First Month"` became a wait.
- smoke: the redundant `stopApp` is gone (`launchApp` stops the app itself) and the relaunch is in
  `retry: maxRetries: 2`. Same `retry` around the XXXL relaunch at the end of expenses.

## Verified
- QA 27 (iOS 27, Release build of `main`, the one QA installed for the previous ticket; that
  ticket changed flows only): smoke + expenses, 3 runs in a row, while `paychecks`, `settings`
  and `income` looped on the `Paycheck Planner Dev` simulator. See the PR for the result lines.
- `maestro check-syntax` on both, `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`: clean.
- Not on the `Release Gate` simulator the ticket names: it belongs to `gate.sh` alone
  (AGENT_LESSONS, HAU-102). Not on Android (Mac worker): the swipes and waits are
  platform-neutral, but the Android gate's next run is the first real check there.
- The launch failure could not be reproduced, so the `retry` path itself never ran.

## Follow-ups
- `paychecks`, `settings` and `pro` relaunch mid-flow without `retry`, and `income` still has two
  `scrollUntilVisible`; give them the same treatment when they show up in a flaky ticket.
- `docs/AGENT_CONTEXT.md`'s follow-up about three `scrollUntilVisible` steps is now income's two only.

## Lessons
- `scrollUntilVisible` + `centerElement` on an element at the end of a scroll view swipes until
  its timeout or its centring retries run out: 2 s per try on an idle Mac, a failure under load.
- The expense form changes height with its choices. After any chip that adds a section, swipe to
  the end again before waiting for the button.
