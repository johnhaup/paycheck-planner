# HAU-337: Maestro suite trimmed to 6 journey flows (2026-11-14)

## What changed (flows and docs only, no app code)
- `.maestro/qa/` went from 24 flows to 6: `smoke`, `paychecks`, `income`, `expenses`, `settings`, `pro`.
  Rule (brain `principles.md`, 2026-11-14): at most 8 flows, one per main journey, no flow per feature;
  Pro, a locale and a text size are steps inside a journey's flow.
- Folded in: free chart's two months, large title + pinned pills, the locked row, tab return, pay date
  toggles / Mark all paid + Undo / pay amount gate, Arabic chart amount (`paychecks`); large title,
  Set-aside gate (`expenses`); Spanish gear label, CSV gate, stacked segments at XXXL (`settings`);
  buy, relaunch, 12-month jump, pay amount sheet, another source, refund + Restore on the XXXL paywall
  with its pinned footer (`pro`). iOS-only steps sit in `runFlow: when: platform: iOS`; only `pro` is
  tagged `[ios]`.
- Dropped (git history has them): the screenshot-only text size and word-wrap flows (`text-size`,
  `pro-text-size`, `source-names`, `pay-date-names`, `pay-date-header`, `-locale`, the amount sheets at
  XXXL), `language-direction`, `month-chart` in Arabic, negative amounts in `chart-amounts`, the mock
  store "no leak" launch, the Currency picker, Income's stacked tiles.
- No flow uses `openLink` any more, so `subflows/approve-deep-link.yaml` and its `onFlowStart` hook are
  gone: an erased gate simulator never raises the "Open in Paycheck Planner?" alert.
- The two Undo retries are one loop each, driven by a flag (`output.undone`), instead of the pair
  written twice.
- `AGENTS.md` and `docs/AGENT_CONTEXT.md`: lines that described the old suite corrected.

## Lessons
- About 5 minutes means about 150 taps: a `tapOn` costs 1.9 s here whatever the load, a launch plus its
  first wait 6 s, a fresh flow 12 s. `waitToSettleTimeoutMs` changed nothing. It took 5m 30s, not 5m 0s.
- `repeat: while: notVisible: X` costs 7 s when X is there (it waits for it to leave), and so does
  `when: visible` on something absent. A flag set by `runFlow: when: visible: X` (instant when X is
  there) and `while: true: ${!output.flag}` skips the retry at no cost on the happy path.
- On Pay Date only the Left over amount is its own element; the pay line is part of the
  "Change … pay amount" button. Compare Left over before and after instead of naming an amount.
- `-AppleLanguages (ar)` on the first Arabic launch shows Arabic text in a left-to-right layout.
