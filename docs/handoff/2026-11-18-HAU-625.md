# HAU-625 Duo: pinned month pills hide under the bar after an unfold

## What changed and why
- Branch is cut from PR #122's branch (HAU-568's commit `35aab0e`), where the bug was found; it is
  not on `main`. Merging this PR also merges that commit.
- Cause was not the content inset (the issue's guess): `pinTop` 0 is right after a fold too
  (scroll `contentInset.top` 0, list height 596 both times). On a fold the Paychecks header is laid out
  once at a passing 115 pt width on its way from 600 to 382 pt; its text wraps and it measures 335 pt
  instead of 306. The HAU-440 floor ("the header never shrinks while the data stays the same") kept
  335 for the session, so the header collapsed 29 pt further and pushed the pills under the bar,
  and the extra blank space below the pills at the top of the list. Each round trip could grow it again.
- `app/(tabs)/(paychecks)/index.tsx`: the floor now also belongs to one header width. At a new width a
  height taller than the old floor is taken as the floor; otherwise (it may be the old `minHeight`
  itself) the floor drops to 0 and the next layout measures the natural height.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`; `.maestro/qa/` on Runner 1 (iPhone 17 Pro, Debug + Metro 8082): 6/6.
- Own Duo simulator (iPhone Duo, iOS 27.1, duoctl v0.2.1, installed with `RUNNER_SIM_UDID=<duo> runner devapp`),
  sample data. Reproduced first: after open, landscape, close, portrait the pills were cut by the bar.
  Debug logs showed the header at width 115 → 335 pt. With the fix: two side→folded round trips
  pinned, then upright pinned and folded again: the pill column frame is y = −20 every time (same as a
  fresh launch), and the screenshots match a fresh launch. The top of the list after folds is right too.
  Duo deleted afterwards.
- Not checked in Light: the app keeps its own theme (simctl appearance does nothing), and the fix has
  no colours. Empty state has no header, unaffected.

## Pitfalls
- A measured "never shrink" floor must be keyed on everything that changes the natural height
  (data, width). Fold/rotation layouts pass through odd widths.
- Metro's console output for the job lands in `~/runner/logs/paycheck-planner/engineer-<ticket>-*-devapp.log`;
  both simulators on the same Metro log there (tell them apart by width).
