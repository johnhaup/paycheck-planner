# HAU-328: core gate flows pass on any date (2026-11-15)

## What changed (one flow step, no app code)
- `.maestro/qa/paychecks.yaml`, "Next month's chart column" step: tapped the first column not
  labelled "(this month)". After the month's last paycheck the free chart is next month and the
  one after (HAU-329), no column is "(this month)", so that tap hit the selected home column and
  "Go to this month" never came. It now taps the column `rightOf` the selected one, by position.
- Checked the other known traps across all six flows: no `point:` taps, no month names, no
  "(this month)" match that must exist. `index: 1` on the free chart is safe since HAU-329
  (always two months). `pro.yaml` taps the third non-current column, never home on any date.
- `docs/AGENT_CONTEXT.md`: QA 27's UDID corrected (the simulator was recreated).
