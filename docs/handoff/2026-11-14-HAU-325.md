# HAU-325: Pay Date Back keeps its chevron on an Arabic device with English picked (2026-11-14)

The bug is in the native-headers work's `modules/layout-direction` (PR 69, on `main` since
`14953bf`; `origin/main` @ `0ad31b6` is merged in here). Needs PR 46's reload to be seen.

## What was wrong (two separate things)
1. No chevron after the in-app reload. react-native-screens sets appearance proxies
   (`UIButton`/`UIView`/`UISearchBar` inside `RNSNavigationBar`) to force-RTL while the app is RTL
   and never resets them: its `direction` prop defaults to `ltr`, so in LTR the native setter never
   runs. The reload keeps the process, the stale proxies force RTL on the back button's inner
   buttons inside an LTR bar, and Auto Layout gives the chevron zero width.
2. After a cold launch the chevron was there but pointed right ("› Back" at the left edge). UIKit
   mirrors the back indicator for the app's iOS language whatever the layout direction trait says.
   It mirrors a custom image the same way; the typesetting-language trait does not help.

## What changed
- `LayoutDirectionModule.swift`: `setRTL` also sets those three proxies (force-RTL, or unspecified
  for LTR, the value of a cold LTR launch). New constant `nativeRTL`: the app's iOS language is RTL.
- `src/constants/backIcon.ts` + `useStackScreenOptions`: only when `nativeRTL` and React Native is
  LTR, `headerBackIcon` is `assets/images/back-chevron-mirrored.png` (drawn pointing right, so iOS's
  mirroring shows "‹"). Every other case keeps the system chevron.

## Verification

`tsc` and lint pass. Release build of this branch plus the picker branch's `app.json` and
`localeStore.ts`, on `Paycheck Planner Dev` (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.0, `5CAD5CFB-…`):
- Arabic device, English picked: "‹ Back" at the left right after the reload and after a cold
  launch, dark and light theme; Back returns to Paychecks.
- English device: system "‹ Back", unchanged. English device, Arabic picked: "رجوع ›" at the right.
- QA ran the full suite and added a cold relaunch to `language-direction.yaml`. Maestro cannot see
  the chevron: read its `direction-pay-date-header` and `direction-pay-date-header-cold` shots.

## Not verified / follow-ups

- English device, Arabic picked, then English again without a cold launch (same stale proxies).
- Our chevron is a bitmap (pill about 3 pt narrower, no Bold Text). If a later iOS stops mirroring
  the indicator it will point right: delete `backIcon.ts`, its spread and the PNGs then.
- Tool: `lldb -p <pid> --batch -o 'expr -l objc -O -- …recursiveDescription'` on the simulator
  process shows frames and `semanticContentAttribute` without Xcode; both causes were found that way.
