# HAU-511 retry around launchApp does not retry a failed simulator launch (2026-11-17)

## What was wrong
The earlier flaky-flow tickets wrapped four mid-flow relaunches in `retry: maxRetries: 2` (smoke's
relaunch, paychecks' Arabic and Japanese relaunches, expenses' XXXL relaunch) to survive a launch
the simulator refuses under gate load. On Maestro 2.11.0 `retry` re-runs its commands only after
a `MaestroException` (a failed assertion or wait). A refused launch surfaces as `CommandFailed:
... Simulator device failed to launch ...` through another path and fails the flow at once, so
the wrappers guarded nothing.

## Change (`.maestro/qa/smoke.yaml`, `paychecks.yaml`, `expenses.yaml` only; the app is untouched)
- The four `retry` wrappers are gone; each relaunch is a plain `launchApp` again, as before the
  wrappers were added. Comments that promised a retried launch are removed.
- The convention line at the top of `smoke.yaml` now says the opposite of what it said: no
  `retry` around a bare `launchApp`, and the gate's re-run of the failed flow covers a failed launch.

## Verified
- Scratch flow on QA 27 (Maestro 2.11.0): `retry: maxRetries: 2` > `launchApp: stopApp: true` on
  an app id that is not installed. `maestro.log` shows one `Launch app ... RUNNING`, then
  `Simulator device failed to launch`, no second attempt and no `Retry 2 times FAILED`.
- QA 27 (iOS 27, Release build installed 2026-11-17 01:07, after the last app-code commit):
  `smoke` 43s, `paychecks` 1m 43s, `expenses` 2m 32s, 3/3 passed in one run.
- `maestro check-syntax` on the three flows, `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`: clean.
- Not on the Release Gate simulators (they belong to `gate.sh`), not on Android (Mac worker; the
  paychecks and expenses relaunches are iOS-only blocks, smoke's is a plain `launchApp`).

## Follow-ups
- linear-runner (not this repo): `gate.sh` re-runs a failed flow once and then files a "Flaky gate
  flow" ticket. A flow whose log has `Simulator device failed to launch` should be treated like a
  dead driver: re-run, no ticket, because no step in a flow can catch it.
- `settings` and `pro` relaunch mid-flow with a plain `launchApp`; leave them that way.

## Lessons
- Maestro 2.11.0 `retry` catches `MaestroException` only (assertions, waits, taps on missing
  elements). It does not retry a launch the simulator refuses. Wrap a launch in `retry` only
  together with a wait that can fail, and expect it to help with a slow start, not a refused one.
- Prove a guard fires before shipping it: both earlier notes say "the `retry` path itself never
  ran". An app id that is not installed makes `launchApp` fail on demand.
