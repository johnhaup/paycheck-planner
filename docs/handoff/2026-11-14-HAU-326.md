# HAU-326: long translations of Mark all paid leave room for the Pay Date title (2026-11-14)

## What changed
- `app/pay-date-detail.tsx`: the navigation bar's "Mark all paid" text is one line, at most 40%
  of the window width (`HEADER_BUTTON_MAX_WIDTH_RATIO`), with `adjustsFontSizeToFit` down to
  0.7 (`HEADER_BUTTON_MIN_FONT_SCALE`); past that it would end in "…".
- Why a width cap and not shorter translations or an icon: the cap holds for all 45 locales and
  any future one without anyone checking string lengths; an icon alone does not say "all", and
  the flows find the button by its text. No strings changed.
- English and other short locales (up to about 20 characters) are under the cap and unchanged.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` clean.
- `Paycheck Planner Dev` (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.0, dark, default text size), branch bundle
  copied over `main.jsbundle` of the installed Release build, `-AppleLanguages (<code>)`, sample
  data, first paycheck. Screenshots read for each:
  - ml, kn, ta, th, te, hu, or (the seven longest, 30 to 33 characters): the title is whole,
    Back is a chevron (ta, th keep the label), the button text is whole inside its pill, smaller
    than body text.
  - de: "Zurück", "Zahltag" and "Alle als bezahlt markieren" all whole (Back had lost its label).
  - en: identical to before (Back, centred "Pay Date", "Mark all paid" at full size).
  - ml, de, en: tapping the button marked every bill paid and showed the Undo toast.
- Not checked: the `.maestro/qa/` suite, light mode, RTL (ur is 26 characters, like de),
  accessibility text sizes, Android, narrower phones (the cap is a share of the width).

## Follow-ups
- The open pull request for "Mark all paid overflows its pill at accessibility text sizes" adds
  `maxFontSizeMultiplier` to the same `AppText`; whichever merges second has a small conflict
  there. Both props belong on it.
- In ml, hu, ta, kn and or the button text is small (roughly 11 to 12 pt). Shorter translations
  in those locales would bring it back to full size; that is translation work, not code.
- The title sits left of centre when the button is wide: UIKit's own behaviour, as in German.

## Pitfall
- A `headerRight` view is sized by its content and UIKit gives it priority over the title and
  Back's label, so any text button in a header needs its own width limit.
