# HAU-786: Android modals show one dismiss control (2026-11-24)

## What changed and why
- Android pushes `presentation: "modal"` screens as full screens, so native-stack drew its back arrow
  next to our X (`renderCloseButton`, HAU-538/HAU-617). `modalOptions` in `app/_layout.tsx` now sets
  `headerBackVisible: false` on Android only. Settings, Income and Expense forms, paywall, Language and
  Currency all spread `modalOptions`, so one line covers the six. iOS is unchanged (sheets had no arrow).
- The X stays on the trailing side as on iOS, so no Maestro `Close` tap changed.
- `.maestro/qa/settings.yaml`: two Android-only `assertNotVisible: "Navigate up"` guards (Settings open,
  Currency picker open).
- Pay date detail (`presentation: "card"`) keeps its arrow; `paychecks.yaml` taps `Back|Navigate up` there.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`.
- Release APK the gate's way (prebuild --clean, `assembleRelease` x86_64) on the emulator (AVD
  `Pixel_9_Pro`, the one running on this machine): `maestro test .maestro/qa/ --exclude-tags ios` 5/5.
- Ad hoc flows: no "Navigate up" on all six modals; title at the leading edge with no gap (English Dark,
  Arabic RTL: title right, X left); system Back and the edge-swipe gesture close Settings, the Income
  form, the paywall (back to Settings) and both pickers (back to Settings, choice unchanged); pay date
  detail still shows "Navigate up".
- Not run: iOS (Linux machine); iOS options are unchanged, so `settings.yaml`'s Android-only blocks
  should be skipped there. Light theme not screenshotted (the header only lost a button).

## Pitfalls
- The Android back arrow's accessibility label is "Navigate up"; Maestro matches it as text.
- Maestro `takeScreenshot` refuses absolute paths outside `--test-output-dir`.
