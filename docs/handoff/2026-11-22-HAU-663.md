# HAU-663 Google Play screenshots (phone, 7" and 10" tablet) and promo video

## What changed
- `scripts/play-assets.mjs` (`bun store:play avds|phone|tablets|frame|promo|sheets`), ported from Apron's Play
  section (HAU-665). Headless emulators on ports 5661/5663/5665 (Pixel_9_Pro, Apron_Tablet_7, Apron_Tablet_10),
  dark mode, demo status bar (9:41, no icons). Framing is expo-local-ship's (`frameScreenshots`), same 7 captions
  as the App Store set (HAU-660). `LOCALES=all` frames every caption locale; default en-US.
- Phone: `ship/screenshots/android/framed/en-US` 01–07 at 1320x2640 (2:1, nothing for Play to crop). Old
  5-scene framed sets of the 49 other locales deleted (stale captions/scenes) and gitignored like the iOS sets.
  Icon and feature graphic re-rendered pixel-identical, so unchanged.
- Tablets: `ship/screenshots-tablet.yaml`, 5 shots each (paychecks, detail, expenses, move, income) framed at
  1440x2560 in `android/tablet-7|tablet-10/framed/en-US`. No `goals` (same view as expenses on a tablet), no
  `amount` (Save half under the keyboard, HAU-719).
- Promo: `ship/play-promo/{setup,1-paychecks,2-detail,3-move,4-goals}.yaml` → `ship/play-promo/en-US/promo.mp4`,
  1080x1920 H.264 High 30 fps, no audio, 25.2 s: poster card, 4 × (title card + scene in the outline), poster.
- `ship/screenshots.yaml`, Android branches only (iOS steps unchanged): back after the amount sheet waits for the
  sheet to close and taps "Navigate up" (a system back only closed the keyboard); Income taps "Mesa Health"
  (Android reads the row's texts apart); Expenses swipes less (70%→36%), the list starts lower under the toolbar.

## How to redo
- APK: `export EXPO_PUBLIC_STORE_DEMO=1` **for both** `bunx expo prebuild --platform android --clean --no-install`
  and `cd android && ./gradlew :app:assembleRelease -PreactNativeArchitectures=x86_64`. Prefixing only the
  prebuild leaves the flag out of the JS bundle: the demo link then does nothing and every flow stops on Welcome.
- `bun store:play phone`, `tablets`, `promo`, `sheets /tmp/pp-sheets`. Each boots and kills its own emulator.

## Verified
- Every framed PNG read at full size, sizes by `magick identify`; promo read at 2 fps and frame by frame around
  the check-offs, `ffprobe` 1080x1920 25.2 s. `maestro check-syntax` on all flows; `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`.
- Not run on iOS: the shared `ship/screenshots.yaml` edits are inside `platform: Android`/`iOS` branches only.

## Bugs filed
- HAU-718 the APK requires `android.hardware.screen.portrait` (Play hides the app from tablets; needs Apron's
  `withOptionalPortrait.js`). HAU-719 amount sheet's Save under the keyboard. HAU-720 names cut at 600 dp.

## Pitfalls
- Tablet Income: the totals tiles are invisible to Maestro on Android (placed outside their row); wait for a source.
- A retried tap on an income row lands on the opened form's amount field and brings up the keyboard.
