# HAU-312: amount sheet titles wrap between words only (2026-11-13)

## What changed
- `AmountSheet` (the body of the pay amount and bill amount sheets) draws its title with
  `AppWordWrapText` at accessibility text sizes (`useAccessibilityTextSize()`), the same switch
  the pay date detail uses for names. Standard sizes keep the plain `AppText`.
- `AppWordWrapText` now accepts the `title` variant as well as `rowTitle` and `body`. Nothing
  else in it changed: a word wider than the sheet shrinks (down to half size) instead of
  breaking at a letter, other words keep full size.
- The title is a user-entered name (source name or payee); the fallbacks (`screens.incomeSource`,
  `screens.expense`) go through the same component, which is harmless.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` clean. Not run on a simulator (engineer runs have none).

## For QA
- Pro (mock store), largest accessibility size, sample data: pay date detail → tap Car
  Registration's amount → scroll the sheet down: title reads "Car" / "Registration", no word cut.
- Same for the pay amount sheet with a source renamed to something long
  ("Brightline Manufacturing and Logistics Holdings Co.").
- Default text size: both sheets unchanged.

## Follow-ups / pitfalls
- The word gap in `AppWordWrapText` is 4pt × font scale, tuned for 15–16pt text; at the 20pt
  title a real space is about 1pt wider. Not worth a per-variant gap unless it looks tight.
- The heading wraps to more lines when a word shrinks or the name is long, so the scroll content
  is taller; the sheet still opens scrolled to the input (HAU-159 behaviour, unchanged).
