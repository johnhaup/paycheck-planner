# HAU-706 Duo upright: Paychecks nav title disappeared after using the sidebar

## What changed and why
- Upright on an unfolded Duo the tabs live in iOS's sidebar (HAU-695). Opening and closing it removed the plain
  `navigationItem.title` of the Paychecks bar for good (back only after a fold or a Settings round trip). Income and
  Expenses have large titles and were not affected.
- Not the planned cause: a diagnostic log showed `useSafeAreaFrame`, `top`, `wide` and `useWindowDimensions` all steady
  (669x951, top 82, wide true) through every open/close, so nothing re-rendered and no option flipped. It is UIKit
  dropping the inline title when the overlay sidebar closes.
- Fix, JS only: on a regular width `app/(tabs)/(paychecks)/_layout.tsx` gives the index screen a `headerTitle` view
  (`AppText` bodyStrong at `font.lg` = 17 pt, `accessibilityRole="header"`, one line, `maxFontSizeMultiplier={1}` like
  the native title). A title view survives the sidebar. Pixel-identical to the native title in a side-by-side crop.
  Compact widths (every iPhone, Duo folded) keep the native title and the collapsing large title untouched.
- The native fallback in the plan (sidebar delegate in the react-native-screens patch) was not needed.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`.
- Scratch `Duo Runner 1` (iPhone Duo, iOS 27.1, duoctl v0.2.1, Runner 1's dev build on Metro 8082), deleted after.
  Before: reproduced (title gone after toggle + tap outside). After, upright with sample data: toggle → tap Paychecks
  row (sidebar stays open, as HAU-695 noted) → tap outside; toggle → tap outside; 3 cycles with a 3 s wait. `duoctl
  elements` lists Heading "Paychecks" after each. Income and Expenses picked from the sidebar keep large titles, back to
  Paychecks keeps its title. Folded (rail, native title), on its side, upright again + sidebar cycle: unchanged/OK.
  Light and dark (app setting), and the empty Welcome state after a reinstall.
- Runner 1 (iPhone 17 Pro): `.maestro/qa/` 6/6 passed (8 min), including the collapsing large title steps.

## Pitfalls
- JS `console.log` from the dev build lands in Metro's event log (`lsof -p <expo pid>` → `.../event-log-501/.../0.jsonl`),
  not in `simctl log stream`.
- `runner upload` answered "Entity not found: Issue" for HAU-706 on 2026-11-22; screenshots were not attached.
- A Fast Refresh re-applies header options and brings the title back, so check a fix after a cold launch.
