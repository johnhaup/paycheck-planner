# HAU-695 Duo unfolded upright: the tab bar straddled the fold

## What changed and why
- Unfolded upright (669x951) iOS draws the floating tab bar centred at the bottom: on the fold, over the last rows of
  both columns. `app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx` now sets NativeTabs `preferredPlacement` (iOS 27, already in
  react-native-screens 4.30.1, so no native change): `"sidebar"` when the window is regular width and taller than wide,
  `"tabBar"` otherwise. Upright, the tabs sit behind a "Toggle sidebar" button at the top left of each tab's nav bar
  and open as iOS's overlay sidebar (~280 pt, left column only). Nothing at the bottom, so both columns' last rows
  scroll fully clear; no inset change was needed.
- Not `"sidebar"` everywhere: on its side iOS docks the sidebar and squeezes both columns (the list and the absolutely
  placed pay date pane overlapped). Not `sidebarAdaptable` alone: `automatic` keeps the centred bottom bar upright.
- Folded and on its side the system's control column (vertical tab rail) is unchanged. Compact iPhones unchanged.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`.
- Scratch `Duo Runner 1` (iPhone Duo, iOS 27.1, duoctl v0.2.1, Runner 1's dev build on Metro 8082), deleted after:

  one-time expense", "See a full year of paychecks" clear); half-open upright; empty Welcome upright; light mode with
  the sidebar open and closed.
- Runner 1 (iPhone 17 Pro): `.maestro/qa/` 6/6 passed (8 min); bottom tab bar looks as before.

## Follow-ups / pitfalls
- Tapping the already-selected tab in the open sidebar does not close it (tap outside or the toggle does). Picking
  another tab closes it. Left as is.
- Not checked: an RTL in-app language upright (the sidebar should come from the right, still off the fold).
- `duoctl tap --label Paychecks` hits the nav bar title, not the sidebar row: tap the sidebar by point.
- axe lists a full-screen "dismiss popup" element in sidebar mode even when the sidebar is closed; it does not block taps.
