# HAU-323: large-title flow passes on any date (2026-11-14)

## What changed
- `.maestro/qa/large-title.yaml` only; no app code. The app behaves correctly: late in the month the
  current month's list is too short to scroll its section title out from under the pinned pills
  (two paychecks left), or to collapse the title at all (one left). The flow assumed a long list.
- The collapse checks (overview gone, pills pinned, centred title, section title gone) now run on
  the chart's **second** month, which is always a whole month. It is picked by position
  (`text: ${MONTH}`, `rightOf: ${MONTH}`), so no step names a month any more ("November",
  "December" were hard-coded and would have failed on 1 December).
- The pinned-pill switch now goes back to the first month (`point: 11%, 15%`) and asserts only that
  it became selected: a short month cannot hold the scroll offset, so the title may expand there.
  The "Go to this month" tap left this flow; `paychecks.yaml` and `month-chart.yaml` cover it.
- On the days after the month's last paycheck the free chart has one month only (the next one), so
  both month-switch blocks are `runFlow: when:` on a second month being there.

## Verified
- New simulator `Paycheck Planner Dev` (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.0), Release `.app` copied from QA 27.
- Old flow, today: fails at the section-title step, header at y 152 (as reported). The issue's
  other failure (collapsed title, gear missing) did not reproduce.
- New flow, today, untouched Release bundle: pass, twice. Screenshots read.
- New flow on simulated dates (branch bundle from `expo export:embed` with a `Date` shim prepended,
  copied over the container's `main.jsbundle`; nothing committed): 2026-11-25 and 11-30 (two
  paychecks left), 11-28 (one left), 12-03 (pill reads "January 2027"), 2027-05-29 (one month in
  the chart, switch blocks skipped): all pass. The first May 29 run failed and led to the `when:`.
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint` clean. Rest of the suite and Android not run (flow is `ios`).

## Follow-ups
- `paychecks.yaml` fails on simulated 2027-05-29: it taps the next month's bar and waits for
  "Go to this month", but the chart has one month then. Any flow that needs a second month does.
- Product: on those days a free user sees one month, not "this month and next" (`buildMonthGroups`
  counts from the calendar month even after it dropped out of the list).

## Lessons
- Maestro `launchApp: clearState` reinstalls the app: the bundle container path changes (look it
  up again), and a swapped `main.jsbundle` survives.
- A date-dependent flow can be tested without touching the clock: prepend a `Date` shim to an
  `export:embed` bundle. Sample data and `buildMonthGroups` both follow it.
- Relative selectors pick a chart column without naming it: `rightOf`/`leftOf` the same regex.
