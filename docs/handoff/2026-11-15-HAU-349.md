# HAU-349: recurring bills can have a due day that picks the paycheck (2026-11-15)

## What changed
- `Expense.dueDay?: number` (1–31). Linked recurring bills only (not set-asides, not one-time).
  When set it replaces `linkSchedule`/`paycheckOrdinal` (the form saves those as undefined).
- `payDates.ts`: `getDueDayPaycheckDate` picks the source's last paycheck dated on or before the
  due day in that calendar month (31 and 29/30 clamp to short months), else the month's first
  paycheck. Months are by actual pay date, so a weekend-shifted semi-monthly check counts in the
  month it is paid. `isExpenseLinkedToPaycheck` checks it before the slot logic;
  `isOncePerMonth` makes the Expenses list total and cadence count a due-day bill once a month.
  `formatDueDay` → `expenses.meta.dueDay` ("Due the 18th"), with the paycheck's date to clamp 31.
- Form (`app/expense-form.tsx`): "Due Day of Month (optional)" number field after the linked
  source chips, shown when a source is picked. While it has a value, How Often / Which Paycheck
  hide. Outside 1–31 → toast `expenses.form.dueDayRange`.
- "Due the 18th" caption under the payee: paycheck card rows (`PaycheckExpenseRow`, via
  `PaycheckExpense.dueDay` from `buildPaycheckItem`), pay date detail rows (`PayDateExpenseRow`,
  new `payDate` prop) and the Expenses list meta. Deleting a source clears `dueDay` too.
- 5 new strings in all 45 locales (translated by the engineer agent; placeholder reuses each
  locale's "e.g." prefix).

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint` clean.
- Scratch bun test (not committed, mocks expo/react-native): bi-weekly from 2026-11-06, due 18 →
  Nov 6, Dec 18, Jan 15, Feb 12, Mar 12; due 5 → Nov 6, Dec 4, Jan 1, Feb 12 (fallback), Mar 12;
  due 31 → last check each month; no due day → every paycheck; semi-monthly 15/31 due 31 → Feb 28.
  "Due the 31st" shows as "Due the 28th" on a February paycheck.
- Simulator `Paycheck Planner Dev`, Release build with a fresh JS bundle swapped in, sample data,
  scratch Maestro flows (not committed): set Internet (Brightline, bi-weekly) to due 18 → on Nov
  20 (no Nov check before the 18th, so the first) and Dec 18, not Dec 4, with "Due the 18th"
  under the payee; Expenses list "Due the 18th · monthly". Changed to 5 → moves to Dec 4.
  Arabic at AccessibilityXXXL: form field, hint and list meta "يستحق يوم 5" wrap fine, RTL.
  Not seen: the pay date detail row, the card row at accessibility sizes, the out-of-range toast.
