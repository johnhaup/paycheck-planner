# HAU-165: large title drawn as a mirror image in ar, he and ur (fix 2026-11-06, merged with main 2026-11-12)

Bug sub-issue of HAU-152, on HAU-152's branch (PR #46).

## Cause
React Native's Fabric scroll view mirrors itself with `scale(-1, 1)` in RTL and flips its content container
back. iOS 26 hosts the large title inside the screen's root scroll view, where UIKit's title gets the mirror
and nothing undoes it. Not the tabs' `direction` prop, the transparent header or react-native-screens.

## App
- The three large-title root lists (Paychecks, Income, Expenses) set `direction: "ltr"` on themselves (no
  transform) and `direction` rtl/ltr from `I18nManager.isRTL` on their content container
  (`LARGE_TITLE_LIST`, `LARGE_TITLE_LIST_CONTENT` in `src/constants/direction.ts`). Content still lays out
  RTL and the nested month chart keeps its own flip.
- The `direction` prop on `NativeTabs` stays: its trait puts the title on the right when the in-app language
  differs from the device.
- react-native-screens PR #4604 (open) counter-transforms the glyphs but keeps UIKit's frame, which here
  sits on the wrong side, so it was not patched in.
- Side effect, accepted (brain `decisions.md`): the vertical scroll indicator on Income and Expenses stays
  on the right in RTL.
- 2026-11-12: merged `origin/main`. Paychecks keeps main's `PinnedHeaderShift` wrapper and
  `StickyHeaderComponent={PinnedHeader}` (HAU-155) with the two styles re-applied to the list.
  `docs/AGENT_CONTEXT.md` is main's; this branch's entries moved to this note and `2026-11-04-HAU-152.md`.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` pass on the merged branch.
- NOT seen on a simulator, before or after the merge. JS only, but the installed build needs HAU-152's
  `app.json` change, so QA needs a Release build of this branch.

## Lessons
- iOS 26 puts the large title inside the screen's root scroll view, and React Native mirrors every RTL
  scroll view with a `scale(-1, 1)` transform (its own content is flipped back), so any UIKit view inside an
  RN scroll view appears mirrored in RTL. A root list under a large title keeps `direction: "ltr"` itself and
  moves RTL to its content container.

## Open follow-ups
- When a react-native-screens release contains PR #4604 (`fix(ios): prevent mirrored RTL large titles`),
  check on an Arabic simulator whether `LARGE_TITLE_LIST`/`LARGE_TITLE_LIST_CONTENT` can go (the PR keeps
  UIKit's frame, so the title may still sit on the wrong side without them).
- The pinned Paychecks header (`PinnedHeader`, a `translateY` on the sticky wrapper inside the RTL content
  container) has never been seen in RTL.
