# HAU-790: iCloud sync switch off and disabled while signed out

**What changed.** `SettingsSyncSection` drew the switch as `state !== "off"`, so `signedOut` and
`unavailable` (no usable iCloud account) showed it on, in green, above "Sign in to iCloud…". Those two
states now show it off and disabled. The hint is unchanged.
- `AppToggle` gained `disabled`: opacity 0.4 (the same as `AppButton`'s disabled state), the Pressable
  ignores taps and gives no haptic, and VoiceOver hears "dimmed" through Pressable's accessibilityState.
- `SettingsToggleRow` passes `disabled` through.
- No state change in the module. `signedOut` means the saved preference is still on (the module's `gate`
  returns `off` before it checks the account), so sync resumes by itself once an account appears. A user
  who turned sync off before signing out sees `off`, and the switch stays enabled.
- `accountChanged`, `needsAppUpdate`, `quotaExceeded` and `error` keep the switch on and enabled. Sync is
  still on there, and each of them has its own hint or action.

**Verified on Runner 1** (iOS 27.0, dev build, no iCloud account). Signed out in Dark and in Light: the
switch is off and dimmed under the sign-in hint. One tap on it leaves the hint unchanged: no "Off…", so
`setEnabled` never ran. `-cloudSyncMock` (signed in): the switch is enabled and on with "Last synced …";
it turns off ("Off…") and back on ("Last synced …"). tsc, lint and `bun test` (36) pass, and so does
`.maestro/qa/settings.yaml`.

**Not verified.** A real iCloud sign-in on the simulator; the module's mock stood in for it. Android has
no Sync section.

**Pitfall.** Turning sync off hides "Turn off and delete iCloud copy", so the switch moves down the
screen. A scratch flow that taps the switch by a fixed point misses it on the second tap.
