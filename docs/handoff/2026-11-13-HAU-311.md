# HAU-311: amount sheets, the hint no longer runs behind the buttons (2026-11-13)

## What changed
- `src/components/AmountSheet.tsx`: the body view is `collapsable={false}`, and one empty
  `<View collapsable={false} />` sits ahead of the `ScrollView`. A comment says why.
- Cause: not the flex layout. Yoga already shrinks the scroll area to what the pinned buttons
  leave. On iOS react-native-screens 4.30 overrides that frame for every `formSheet`
  (`RNSScreen.mm`, `tryFindDescendantScrollView`). It looks for a scroll view in two places:
  1. the direct subviews of `RNSScreenContentWrapper`. Fabric flattened our body view, so the
     ScrollView was one of them and got the whole sheet's size
     (`coerceChildScrollViewComponentSizeToSize`);
  2. the chain of first subviews below the screen ("Fallback 1"), which gets the sheet's frame.
- Round 1 (`b6cf71e`) added only the empty view. That blocks search 2 but not search 1: the
  ScrollView moved to index 1, was still sized to the sheet, and the padding went too (QA fail).
- Round 2: the body is no longer flattened, so the wrapper's only subview is the body (search 1
  finds nothing), and the empty view is the body's first subview (search 2 ends there). The
  scroll view keeps the frame the layout gives it. Both are needed.

## Pitfall
- In a `formSheet`, react-native-screens resizes a ScrollView that is a direct subview of the
  content wrapper (after flattening!) or first-child-of-first-child below the screen. To keep a
  ScrollView beside pinned content: non-collapsable parent, and a non-scroll first sibling.
