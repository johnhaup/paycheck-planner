# HAU-303: Mark all paid fits its header pill at accessibility text sizes (2026-11-13)

## What changed
- `app/pay-date-detail.tsx`: the navigation bar's "Mark all paid" text has
  `maxFontSizeMultiplier={1}` (`HEADER_MAX_FONT_MULTIPLIER`). It used to scale with Dynamic Type
  (up to 3.57x) inside iOS's fixed-height glass pill, so it stuck out of the pill, squeezed the
  "Pay Date" title away and removed Back's label.
- Why 1 and not a larger cap: the bar's other contents (Back, the title) are UIKit's and do not
  scale at all, and the pill's height is fixed. The button now looks the same at every text size,
  which is what a native bar button does. It is the only text button in a header; the others are
  icons with a fixed size.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` clean.
- Dev build on `Paycheck Planner Dev` (iOS 27, Metro from this worktree), launched with
  `-UIPreferredContentSizeCategoryName UICTContentSizeCategoryAccessibilityXXXL`, sample data,
  first paycheck: screenshot shows "Back", the centred "Pay Date" title and "Mark all paid" inside
  its pill; tapping it showed the Undo toast.
- Not checked: other accessibility sizes (same rendering by construction, the cap is 1), a Release
  build, other languages, Android.

## For QA
- `.maestro/qa/pay-date-names.yaml` already opens this screen at AccessibilityXXXL and takes
  `pay-date-names-header`; that screenshot now shows the fixed bar. No flow was edited.

## Follow-ups
- Long translations of "Mark all paid" (ml, kn, th, ta, te are 85 to 95 characters wide in code
  units, de/fr are long too) are not capped in width and probably crowd the title at every text
  size, including the default. Not looked at here; it is independent of text size.
- A capped bar button would normally offer iOS's Large Content Viewer on long press
  (`accessibilityShowsLargeContentViewer`); not added, untested with a Pressable in `headerRight`.
