# HAU-440: Paychecks scroll and jumps without jitter (2026-11-16)

## What changed and why
- Jitter crossing a month: the pinned header sits above every row, and its height followed the
  month on top (the shortfall line showed only for a short paycheck outside that month). Crossing
  a month resized it, every row moved, and the month could flip back (up, then down). Now
  (`app/(tabs)/(paychecks)/index.tsx`) the shortfall line shows whenever a paycheck ahead is short
  (pointing outside the month on screen when it can), and the header keeps the tallest height it
  has measured for the current data (`headerFloor`, `minHeight`; reset when `months` changes).
- Chart taps, Today and the shortfall line scroll the list with UIKit's animation (`jumpTo(index,
  true)`) and the header comes out with `withTiming` instead of popping. The 50 ms timed repeats
  that made the old jumps stutter stay only for the unseen jumps (launch, tab return, Erase all
  data); an animated jump is run again once from where it stopped, on `onMomentumEnd` (iOS sends it
  when an animated `scrollTo` ends; a repeat at the same offset is a no-op), at most twice.
- `MonthBarChart`: one tinted pill slides to the selected month (`selectedPill`, translateX,
  sign-flipped in RTL) and each bar cross-fades between grey and green (`interpolateColor`); a
  column's own pill fill fades out while it is selected, so the slide shows over it.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint` clean; `.maestro/qa/paychecks.yaml` and `pro.yaml` pass (Dev sim, Release).
- Release JS bundle swapped into the Dev sim's Release build, Pro on the mock store, sample data
  plus a $4,000 one-time bill on Jan 15 (a short paycheck), recorded with `simctl io recordVideo`,
  read at 10-15 fps. `main`: crossing Dec→Jan drops the shortfall line and the rows jump with it.
  Branch: same scroll moves evenly through the switch; chart tap (April), Today and a Feb tap
  scroll animated, header expands with them, no snap after; pill slides, bars cross-fade; Arabic
  places the pill on the right month.

## Pitfalls
- Maestro `tapOn: "<Month>.*left"` while the header is collapsed taps the column's centre, which
  sits under the status bar (the bars fade but keep their frame), so iOS scrolls to the top. Tap a
  collapsed pill by point.
- An animated jump must not be repeated on a timer: each call restarts UIKit's animation.

## Follow-ups
- The header floor resets only when `months` changes; after lowering the text size the header can
  keep empty room under the meta line until the data next changes.
- Not seen on a real device (120 Hz ProMotion); the simulator records at 60 fps at best.
