# HAU-667 iPhone Duo: full width in two columns, split at the fold

## What changed and why
- The centred 600 pt column (HAU-554, HAU-569) is gone: Apple's Duo guide says not to ship one. `useGutters` is
  now safe area + 20 pt only, and the react-native-screens patch lost its `RNSScreenStack.mm` hunk (native change).
- One breakpoint, `isRegularWidth` (600 pt) in `src/constants/layout.ts`; `useColumns()` gives the two columns of a
  regular-width window, split at the window's middle with a 32 pt gap (`COLUMN_GAP`), so the fold runs between them.
- Paychecks: the list keeps to the first column (content `paddingRight`), the picked paycheck is beside it
  (`PayDateDetail`, extracted from `app/pay-date-detail.tsx`, `embedded`; "Mark all paid" sits in its summary).
  A tap on a card picks (outlined card, `paycheckSelectionStore`) instead of pushing. Unfolding with the pay date
  screen open closes it into the split view on the same paycheck. No large title on a regular width, so `pinTop` is
  0 there and the Duo never has a large title on Paychecks (the `DUO_LARGE_TITLE_HEIGHT` fallback is gone).
- Income: totals in the first column, sources in the second. The list's content is padded into the second column
  and the tiles are absolutely placed back into the first: styles only, the list is untouched by a fold.
- Expenses: a line holds two bills (`ExpenseLine`), a section runs down the first column then the second; the
  summary is in the first column and the filter in the second. Welcome and `AppEmptyState`: text | buttons.
- Modals and sheets stay one column, now as wide as iOS makes them.
- `scripts/duo-screenshots.mjs`: seven scenes on both screens. Cover: `ship/screenshots.yaml` by Maestro on the
  folded Duo. Inner: duoctl. `DUO_SIMULATOR`, `METRO_URL` (dev build on a demo Metro). See `ship/screenshots/duo/README.md`.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`. Own Duo (iOS 27.1, duoctl v0.2.1, dev build on Metro): every screen and
  sheet folded, on its side, half-open, upright, half-open upright, folded again; fold with each open; empty states.
- Runner 1 (iPhone 17 Pro, dev build on Metro): `.maestro/qa/` 6/6 passed (8 min); Paychecks unchanged (large title,
  one column). Store set captured and framed again on a fresh `Duo Runner 1` (iOS 27.1), every image read.

## Follow-ups / pitfalls
- Amount sheet open on the unfolded Duo upright, then half-folded: the number pad grows (top 645 → 567 pt) and iOS
  leaves the sheet where it was, so the pad covers the lower half of Save (still tappable at its top). Opening the
  sheet half-folded places it right. A blur and re-focus on keyboard growth did not move it; not shipped.
- `ship/screenshots.yaml` (cover): waits for Income before tapping a source (tap dropped) and scrolls to the card's
  Move a bill row (below the cover's fold). Not re-run on the 6.9" iPhone; both steps are no-ops there. duoctl lists
  Save as a GenericElement, so the script taps it by label only. Inner Expenses shot ends half a line under the bar.
- Android foldables get the same two columns from the same breakpoint; not run here (Linux worker tickets).
- Nothing uploaded to App Store Connect; expo-local-ship's uploader has no `APP_IPHONE_DUO` set.
- Once, on the folded Duo, Expenses first mounted straight after the amount sheet with no right inset until the
  next scroll (content under the status column). Not reproduced by hand in the same order.
- The number pad's keys are not accessibility elements: the capture script taps them by point (`PAD`).
- Income's tiles are placed 51 pt down to sit level with the sources card; at large text sizes the section title
  is taller and they sit a little high.
