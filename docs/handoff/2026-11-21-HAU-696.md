# HAU-696 Paychecks: rows cut off flat under the pinned month header

## What changed and why
- The pinned header (month pills, Go Pro) already had an opaque `screenBackground`. What was missing was an edge:
  rows were cut off flat at its bottom with nothing to show where the header ends, so a half row (Internet, the top
  of the Savings card) looked like a rendering glitch. Most visible on the Duo on its side, where there is no large
  title or navigation bar above the pills.
- `app/(tabs)/(paychecks)/index.tsx`: a hairline (`colors.border`, `StyleSheet.hairlineWidth`) along the header's
  bottom edge. Its opacity follows `pinShift`: 0 while the header scrolls with the list, full once 8 pt of rows are
  under it (`PINNED_DIVIDER_FADE`). The same on every iPhone, like a UIKit bar's edge once content is under it.
  `pointerEvents="none"`. No header in the empty/welcome state, so nothing changes there.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`.
- Runner 1 (iPhone 17 Pro, dev build on Metro 8082): dark and light, at the top (no line) and scrolled with rows under
  the pills (line). `.maestro/qa/smoke.yaml` and `paychecks.yaml` passed.
- Scratch `Duo Runner 1` (iPhone Duo, iOS 27.1, duoctl v0.2.1), deleted after: on its side, sample data, at the top
  (no line) and scrolled twice (line under the pills across the left column only, rows pass under it); folded with
  the list scrolled (line under the pills). Duo checked in dark only; the colour is the same token as light iPhone.

## Pitfalls
- `duoctl swipe` takes `--duration`, not a fifth positional argument.
- `runner devapp` for a second simulator must run from the worktree (from elsewhere: "bun install failed").
