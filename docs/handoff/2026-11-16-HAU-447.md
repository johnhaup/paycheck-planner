# HAU-447 Google Play screenshots, icon and feature graphic

## What changed
- `ship/screenshots/android/`: 5 raw shots from a Pixel_9_Pro emulator (API 35, demo-mode status
  bar, dark mode), 5 framed 1320x2640 shots for each of the 50 caption locales (250 PNGs),
  `icon-512.png` and `feature-graphic.png` (1024x500). Made by
  `expo-local-ship screenshots capture --android` and `frame --android`. Nothing was uploaded.
- `ship/screenshots.yaml`: `appId: ${APP_ID}` (the tool passes the bundle id or the package); waits
  for the Paychecks screen before `openLink` (Android drops a link that arrives during a cold start,
  the app stayed on the empty state); opens the paycheck by the card's label
  (`[A-Z]{3}, [0-9]+, Mesa Health.*`) and waits for "This paycheck". The old `.*Mesa Health.*` /
  `Pay Date` steps were stale on both platforms. No `back` step was needed: the flow never goes back.
- Demo builds are Pro: `paycheck-planner://demo` sets `isPro` and the launch entitlement check is
  skipped when `EXPO_PUBLIC_STORE_DEMO=1` (`IS_STORE_DEMO` in `src/services/pro.ts`). Without it the
  shots showed the "Go Pro" card instead of the year chart and the paywall instead of the amount
  sheet, unlike the App Store shots (taken before Pro existed). Dead code in every normal build.

## How it was made / verified
- APK the gate's way: `EXPO_PUBLIC_STORE_DEMO=1 bunx expo prebuild --platform android --clean
  --no-install`, then `./gradlew :app:assembleRelease -PreactNativeArchitectures=x86_64`, `adb install`.
- Emulator booted by hand, headless (`emulator -avd Pixel_9_Pro -port 5584 -no-window -gpu
  swiftshader_indirect`, then `adb shell cmd uimode night yes` for a dark keyboard); the tool finds
  a running AVD by name. Its own boot opens a window, and `capture --build` uses `expo run:android`.
- Read all five raw shots and framed samples in en-US, ar-SA, ur-PK, he, ja, zh-Hans, ko, hi, ta-IN,
  th, de-DE; sizes checked with `magick identify`. `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` pass.

## Linux worker setup done for this (no sudo, all under ~)
- `~/.local/bin/magick` (ImageMagick 7.1.2-36 portable, in `~/.local/opt/`), `~/.local/bin/rsvg-convert`
  (from the `librsvg2-bin` deb), AVD `Pixel_9_Pro` (android-35 google_apis x86_64, 4 GB RAM).
- The framer asks for "System Font" (San Francisco on the Mac). Here
  `~/.config/fontconfig/conf.d/60-system-font-inter.conf` maps it to Inter (`~/.local/share/fonts/inter`),
  then Noto Sans. Captions are Inter, not SF: close, not identical to the App Store set.
