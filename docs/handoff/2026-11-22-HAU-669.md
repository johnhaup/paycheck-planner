# HAU-669: Android foldables, Pixel 9 Pro Fold (2026-11-22)

## What changed and why
- Layout: no new rule. The one breakpoint (`isRegularWidth`, 600) already splits the unfolded Fold (852 x 883 dp)
  at its middle, where the hinge runs; folded (443 dp) is one column. Plate Weight's "near square" gate (HAU-668)
  was not ported: here 7"/10" tablets already have two columns and HAU-663's Play tablet shots show them, so the gate
  would have made those shots wrong. Comments in `src/constants/layout.ts` and `useColumns` say so.
- `src/hooks/useScrollKeptOnResize.ts` (Android only, port of Plate Weight's): unfold then fold sent Expenses back
  to its top. Used by Income, Expenses, Settings, Language/Currency (`SettingsChoiceList`), paywall, both forms.
  Paychecks keeps its place through its own jump/reflow logic; not touched.
- `AppText`: on Android a text with `adjustsFontSizeToFit` shrinks while a fold resizes the window and never grows
  back (paycheck and income amounts drawn at about a quarter of their size). It now remounts that one text on a
  new window width (`RefittedText`).
- `app/_layout.tsx`: HAU-716's transparent Expense form header is now iOS only. On Android the form started under
  the bar (no automatic content inset), Bill | Set-aside could not be tapped, and `expenses.yaml` failed.
- No manifest change: the built manifest's MainActivity has `configChanges` with orientation, screenLayout,
  screenSize, smallestScreenSize (no `resizeableActivity`; the default is true for our target SDK).

## Verified
- `fold` AVD (API 35), Release APK: every tab, Welcome and the empty states, pay-date detail, Expense form with typed
  text, Settings, Language, paywall. Each one unfolded, folded and unfolded again, plus tabletop (hinge 90°, rotated:
  the portrait-locked app stays upright and split). Same PID throughout. Scroll, typed text and the stack are kept.
  `uiautomator dump` unfolded: only the bottom nav's "Income" tab crosses x = 1038 px.
- `runner` AVD, Release APK: `maestro test --exclude-tags ios .maestro/qa/` 5/5 (5 min).
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`.

## Follow-ups and pitfalls
- Tabletop layout (posture) is not exposed by RN; not built. Bottom nav "Income" sits on the hinge (left alone).
- `screencap` needs `-d <id>` on the fold (`dumpsys SurfaceFlinger --display-id`: inner first, outer second).
- Expense form's Save sits under the keyboard unfolded: HAU-719, already filed.
