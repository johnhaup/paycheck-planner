# HAU-163: PRO controls stay on screen at accessibility text sizes (2026-11-13)

## What changed
- `AppSegmentedControl`: at accessibility sizes (`useAccessibilityTextSize()`) the options are one
  per line instead of side by side. Labels wrap instead of ending in "…", and a PRO badge that
  does not fit beside its label drops under it, inside the segment. Segments grow with their
  text (36pt minimum). This is the shared control, so the Settings theme picker, the Expenses
  filter and the expense form's "How often" / "Which paycheck" pickers stack too.
- Past paychecks (`app/history.tsx`): the "See earlier months" row wraps. The label takes at most
  the row's width and breaks between words; the badge and chevron move to a second centred line
  when they do not fit beside it. Applies at every size, so a long translation wraps as well.
- Standard text sizes: no layout change (segments still equal-width, 36pt high, one line).
- Inline PRO badges still scale with the text (HAU-131's rule: only text in fixed boxes is capped).

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` clean. Not run on a simulator (no engineer simulator
  exists for this app), so the layout at Accessibility XL/XXXL is unseen.

## For QA
- Free tier, Accessibility XL and XXXL. Expenses → + : "Bill" and "Set-aside" are two full-width
  rows, "Set-aside" whole, PRO badge inside the second row; tapping it opens the paywall.
- Paychecks → Past paychecks, scroll to the end: "See earlier months" whole, nothing cut at the
  left edge, badge and chevron on screen; tapping opens the paywall.
- Also at those sizes: Settings theme picker (System / Light / Dark) and the Expenses filter are
  stacked and still switch. Default size: all of these look as before.
- Flows that tap segment labels by text should be unaffected; a screenshot step in
  `text-size.yaml` for the two screens would protect this.

## Pitfall
- Yoga only shrinks an item within its line: `flexWrap: "wrap"` plus `flexShrink: 1` on the text
  gives "wrap the label when it is alone on a line, otherwise move the badge down".
