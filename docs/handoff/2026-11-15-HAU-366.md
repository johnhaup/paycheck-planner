# HAU-366: German Expenses filter and Income schedule cut off (2026-11-15)

## What changed
- `AppSegmentedControl`, side by side: a segment was `flex: 1` (three equal thirds), so a long
  label was cut ("Wiederkehre…" between "Alle" and "Einmalig"). Now each segment starts at its
  label's width (`flexBasis: "auto"`) and shares the spare room equally (`flexGrow: 1`), the way
  UIKit's `apportionsSegmentWidthsByContent` does. A label that still does not fit shrinks (to half
  size at most, `adjustsFontSizeToFit` now at every size, not only stacked) instead of ending in
  "…". Shared control, so Settings' Appearance and the expense form get the same behaviour; in
  English the segments are slightly unequal in width, nothing else changes. Stacked layout at
  accessibility sizes unchanged.
- `IncomeSourceRow`: the schedule line ("Zweimal monatlich · 15. & 30.") had `numberOfLines={1}`
  at standard sizes; it now wraps at every size. Chose wrapping over shorter wording: it fixes all
  45 locales at once and needs no translation changes.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint` clean.
- Release build on `Paycheck Planner Dev`, sample data, scratch Maestro flow (not committed)
  relaunching with `-AppleLanguages`: de, ta, ml, fi, da show every filter label whole at full
  size; en unchanged apart from widths. Income: de "Zweimal monatlich · 15. &" / "30." on two
  lines, fi and da wrap the same way, ml wraps the bi-weekly line. ta's Income screenshot came
  out blank (timing), so ta Income was not seen.

## Follow-ups
- The wrap can split "15. &" from "30."; non-breaking spaces between the days (in the locale
  strings or `describePaySchedule`) would keep them together. Cosmetic, not done here.

## Pitfalls
- Side by side, the label needs `flexShrink: 1` inside the segment (a row Pressable), or the
  text keeps its full width and overflows the segment instead of shrinking.
