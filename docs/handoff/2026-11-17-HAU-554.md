# HAU-554 iPhone Duo, folded and unfolded, with App Store screenshots

## What changed and why
- `src/hooks/useGutters.ts`: side padding for every screen's content = safe area + centring margin
  (content and gutters stop at 600 pt) + the old 20 pt. The Duo keeps a status column at the right
  (84 pt inset folded 466x678 and unfolded on its side 951x669; upright 669x951 it is an 82 pt top
  inset), and content ran under it and stretched edge to edge. On other iPhones it returns 20/20.
  Used by the three tabs, pay date, Settings, paywall, both forms, the choice lists, `AppFab` and
  `MonthBarChart` (its viewport is the content column plus a gutter each side, not the window).
- Modals (`settings`, `paywall`, `income-form`, `expense-form`, `language`, `currency`) wrap their
  content in a `SafeAreaProvider`: unfolded on its side iOS centres a modal clear of the status
  column, so the window's insets are wrong inside it. Bottom sheets are unchanged.
- Paychecks: on a wide window with a top inset the bar is inside the inset, so the pinned header
  pins at `insets.top` (was 44 pt low). A fold no longer re-runs the opening jump (`pinTopRef`), so
  the list keeps its place; 400 ms after a reflow the header's collapse is recomputed.
- `scripts/duo-screenshots.mjs` (`bun store:screenshots:duo`), `ship/screenshots/duo/` (README there):
  5 scenes, raw + framed en-US at 1398x2034 and 2007x2853, unfolded landscape raw 2853x2007.
  `tsconfig.json` excludes `scripts` (it imports expo-local-ship's sources).
