# HAU-568 Duo: large title no longer covers the pinned month pills after a fold

## What changed and why
- `app/(tabs)/(paychecks)/_layout.tsx`: Paychecks has a large title only while the top safe-area inset
  is above 0 (John's approach). Folded and unfolded on its side (top inset 0) iOS hid the whole 82 pt
  large-title bar on scroll and brought it back expanded after a fold, over the pills pinned for 44 pt.
  The standard bar there is always shown, 82 pt, and the list's frame starts below it (y 82).
  A standard iPhone and the Duo unfolded upright keep the large title.
- `app/(tabs)/(paychecks)/index.tsx`: `pinTop` is 0 when the top inset is 0: the list starts below
  the bar, so the header pins against the list's own top edge, as on Android.
- Same file, round 2: the HAU-440 `headerFloor` (the header never shrinks while the data is the same)
  now also resets when the window width changes. Part way through a fold the header measures taller
  (306 → 335 or 366 pt folded), the floor kept it as `minHeight`, and the extra height became an empty
  strip under the chart and a bigger collapse distance that pinned the pills 30–60 pt up under the bar.
  Round 1 missed this: it showed only after folded → side → folded with the app already running.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`; `.maestro/qa/` on Runner 2 (iPhone 17 Pro, Debug + Metro 8083): 6/6.
- Own Duo (iPhone Duo, iOS 27.1, duoctl v0.2.1), dev build on Metro. Before the floor fix: folded →
  side → folded left the pills under the bar (temporary readout in the nav title: header 306 → 366,
  list frame and pinTop unchanged). After: header stays 306 over three round trips; pinned,
  folded → side → upright → folded twice, pills fully below the bar each time, also after a further
  scroll. Top of the list in every pose. Dark and Light. Duo deleted afterwards.
- iPhone 17 Pro: large title at the top, collapses with the pills pinned below it (screenshots).

## Follow-ups / pitfalls
- Going from side (or folded) to upright, the large title comes back collapsed (inline) until the
  list is pulled down: the list keeps its offset. Nothing is covered.
- `duoctl elements` frames inside the list are not screen positions (the pinned header read y −79.7
  in every pose, right or wrong). Read screenshots, or put numbers in the nav title temporarily.
- `axe swipe` fails on the Duo ("Motion sample reports invalid angle"); use `duoctl swipe`.
- Fold with the app already running and scrolled, then check, repeated: a fresh launch per pose
  hides state a fold leaves behind.
