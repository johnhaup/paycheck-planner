# HAU-568 Duo: large title no longer covers the pinned month pills after a fold

## What changed and why
- `app/(tabs)/(paychecks)/_layout.tsx`: Paychecks has a large title only while the top safe-area inset
  is above 0 (John's approach). Folded and unfolded on its side (top inset 0) iOS hid the whole 82 pt
  large-title bar on scroll and brought it back expanded after a fold, over the pills pinned for 44 pt.
  With the standard bar it is always there and always the same height. A standard iPhone and the Duo
  unfolded upright (82 pt top inset) keep the large title.
- `app/(tabs)/(paychecks)/index.tsx`: `pinTop` is 0 when the top inset is 0. Without a large title
  react-native-screens lays the screen out below the opaque bar, not under it, so the header pins
  against the list's own top edge, as on Android. Keeping 44 there left an ~80 pt empty strip.
- Income and Expenses are unchanged (nothing pins under their titles).

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`; `.maestro/qa/` on Runner 1 (iPhone 17 Pro, Debug + Metro 8082): 6/6.
- Own Duo `Paycheck Planner HAU-568 Duo` (iPhone Duo, iOS 27.1, duoctl v0.2.1, dev build installed with
  `RUNNER_SIM_UDID=<duo> runner devapp`). Bug reproduced first with the large title forced on.
  With the fix, sample data, scrolled with the pills pinned: folded → side → upright → folded, pills
  fully below the bar each time, title and gear stay. Same round trip at the top of the list. Upright
  at the top shows the large title. Light and Dark; empty state folded. Screenshots read.
- iPhone 17 Pro: one screenshot flow on `main`'s code and this branch's: top of list 0 differing
  pixels; pinned, the bar, title and pills are identical (diff starts below the header, swipe momentum).

## Follow-ups / pitfalls
- Going from side (or folded) to upright at the top of the list, the large title comes back collapsed
  (inline) until the list is pulled down: the list keeps its offset. Nothing is covered.
- `true || useSafeAreaInsets()` as a quick toggle short-circuits a hook and trips React's hook-order
  warning; read the hook into a const first.
