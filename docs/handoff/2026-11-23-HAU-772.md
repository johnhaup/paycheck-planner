# HAU-772 Income form: error toast covers the sheet title and X

## What changed and why
- The income form refused a save with a `toastError`, which sonner-native draws at the top of the
  window, over the sheet's "Income source" title and X, and no field was marked. It now does what the
  expense form does since HAU-700: one `error` state naming the field, `AppInput`'s `error` (red
  outline, message under the field), the warning haptic, focus on the field, cleared on edit.
- `AppInput` takes `invalid` (outline only, no message). The two semi-monthly pay-day inputs sit in a
  flex row and `AppInput` returns a fragment, so an `error` message would become a row child. The
  form outlines the first out-of-range day and draws "Pay days must be between 1 and 31." once
  under the row, above the hint. With neither `error` nor `invalid`, `AppInput` renders as before.
- No new strings. `toastError` stays; other screens still use it.

## Verified (Runner 1, iOS 27, dev build on Metro 8082)
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint` clean.
- Scratch Maestro flows, dark (add form) and light (edit form on sample data): empty name, then
  empty and `0` amount, then semi-monthly day 1 = 40 and day 2 = 0 each show their message, outline
  and focus with the title and X visible and no toast; typing clears each; the valid save closes.
- Largest accessibility size: name and pay-days cases outline correctly, the "and" row keeps its
  alignment.
- `.maestro/qa/income.yaml` and `expenses.yaml` pass (expenses failed once on its keyboard swipe
  before "Update expense", unrelated; passed on rerun).
- Android not checked (Mac, no emulator).

## Follow-ups
- At the largest text sizes the message under a focused field can sit behind the keyboard: the
  KeyboardAwareScrollView brings the input, not its message, above the keyboard (also true of the
  expense form). A larger `bottomOffset` while an error shows would fix both.
- `paycheck-amount`, `expense-amount` and `OneTimeExpenseForm` still use the top toast.

## Pitfalls
- Maestro on these forms at XXXL: `scrollUntilVisible` and taps on labels land on the number pad
  and type digits. Pick chips before focusing a number field, and swipe above the keyboard.
