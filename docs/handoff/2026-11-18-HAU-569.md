# HAU-569 Duo unfolded: large title lines up with the content column

## What changed and why
- `patches/react-native-screens@4.30.1.patch`, new hunk in `RNSNavigationController viewDidLayoutSubviews`
  (`ios/RNSScreenStack.mm`): a stack whose bar is visible adds the space beside the 600 pt column to its
  own `additionalSafeAreaInsets` (left and right, `(safe width - 600) / 2`, 0 on every other iPhone).
  UIKit places the large title and the bar buttons by the bar's safe area, so they now sit on the
  column's edges. `useGutters` reads the same safe area: its centring margin becomes 0 there and the
  content stays exactly where it was. No JS change apart from a comment in `useGutters.ts`.
- Side effects, all intended: the gear / sort / search / "Mark all paid" buttons move in to the column's
  trailing edge, pushed Pay date gets the same, and a modal wider than 600 pt (Settings, paywall on the
  side: about 650 pt) has its Close button 25 pt further in, on the content's edge.
- The 600 is in two places (the patch and `COLUMN_MAX_WIDTH`); each comment names the other.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run lint`; `.maestro/qa/` on Runner 2 (iPhone 17 Pro, Debug + Metro 8081): 6/6.
- iPhone Duo simulator (iOS 27.1, duoctl v0.2.1), frames from `duoctl elements`: on its side title
  x 153.7 = content x 153.7, gear's right edge on the column; upright title 54.7 = content 54.5.
  Paychecks (empty, sample data, scrolled with pinned pills), Income, Expenses, Pay date, Settings,
  paywall; dark and light; Arabic (title's right edge 713.3 = content 713.7); folded (unchanged);
  folded → side → upright with a screen open. Screenshots read.
- iPhone 17 Pro: empty Paychecks before/after the hunk differs by 576 px of 2.9 M (`magick compare`).

## Pitfalls
- On iOS 27 the large title ignores `directionalLayoutMargins` of the bar, of the screen's view and
  `systemMinimumLayoutMargins`; margins on the bar's content view crash; `additionalSafeAreaInsets` on
  the screen (child) moves the content but not the bar. Only the navigation controller's own safe area
  (or an ancestor's) moves the title.
- Computing the width from the controller's own `safeAreaInsets` while setting its additional insets
  loops (white screen, main thread stuck). Measure in the superview.
- After a patch edit, `rm -rf node_modules/react-native-screens && bun install` and diff: that is the
  check that the patch file applies.
