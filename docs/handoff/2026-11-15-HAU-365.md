# HAU-365: Arabic, Hebrew and Urdu devices get an RTL layout on `main` (2026-11-15)

The fix lived on the parked RTL branch (PR #46, commits `1bbefb6` and `ee219f0`) and never reached `main`.
That branch's `app`/`src`/`app.json` diff is ported here onto current `main`. Its handoff notes are
`2026-11-04-HAU-152.md` and `2026-11-12-HAU-165.md` on that branch.

## What changed and why
- `app.json`: `expo-localization` `supportedLocales` lists all 45 locales, which writes
  `CFBundleLocalizations`. Without it iOS treats the app as English-only and RN stays LTR on an Arabic device.
- `localeStore`: when the in-app language's direction differs from `I18nManager.isRTL` (after a pick, or after
  hydration), it calls `allowRTL`/`forceRTL` and `reloadAppAsync`. `app/_layout.tsx` already calls
  `LayoutDirection.setRTL` on each JS start, so the native headers follow the reload.
- Things that don't mirror by themselves: `AppToggle`'s thumb, chevrons (`FORWARD_CHEVRON` in
  `src/constants/direction.ts`, now also on the shortfall line, `ProUnlockRow` and both `SettingsLinkRow`
  chevrons), the swipe actions (`startAction`/`endAction`) and the native tab bar (`direction` prop).
- The large-title lists (Paychecks, Income, Expenses) keep `direction: "ltr"` themselves and set RTL on their content
  container. Without that, iOS 26+ draws the large title mirrored at the left edge.
  On Paychecks this applies to the new continuous `PaycheckList` (`listStyle`, `LIST_CONTENT_STYLE`).
- The "Past paychecks" link and `app/history.tsx` from the old branch no longer exist on `main`, so they were dropped.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` pass. The built Info.plist has 45 `CFBundleLocalizations`.
- Release build (`expo run:ios --configuration Release --device generic --output`) on `Paycheck Planner Dev`
  (`E802AB54-…`, iOS 27.0), with scratch Maestro flows that are not committed:
  - Fresh install, `-AppleLanguages (ar)` `-AppleLocale ar_AE`: Welcome, Paychecks (expanded and collapsed with
    the pinned pills), Income and Expenses are all mirrored. Tab order runs right to left, the large title is on
    the right and reads correctly, the date badge is on the right, the chart starts at the right and the FAB is
    on the left. `الرواتب` is `rightOf` `الدخل`.
  - English device, Arabic picked in Settings > Language: the app reloads into RTL. Picking English again
    reloads back to LTR (`Income` is `rightOf` `Paychecks`).
  - `.maestro/qa/` `smoke`, `paychecks` (has the Arabic launch) and `settings`: 3/3 pass. Not run: `income`,
    `expenses`, `pro`. Not checked: Hebrew and Urdu (same code path), the swipe actions and
    the toggle in RTL, light mode.

## Follow-ups / pitfalls
- Native change: a JS bundle swap onto an older `.app` shows nothing; QA needs a build of this branch.
- `.maestro/qa/paychecks.yaml`'s Arabic step asserts only text. A direction check would be
  `text: "الرواتب"` `rightOf: "الدخل"` (QA owns flows).
- Accepted side effect from that branch: Income and Expenses keep their scroll indicator on the right in RTL.
- When react-native-screens ships PR #4604, re-check whether the large-title list styles can go.
