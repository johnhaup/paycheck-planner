# HAU-350: Bills that repeat every other paycheck, quarterly or yearly (2026-11-15, 11-16)

## What changed
- `LinkSchedule` gains `every-other-paycheck`, `quarterly`, `semiannual`, `yearly`; `Expense`
  gains `repeatStart` (YYYY-MM-DD): the anchor pay date for every other paycheck, the 1st of a
  month it lands in for 3/6/12 months. Repeats run both ways (Pro history follows the cycle).
  Old bills have no `repeatStart` and keep their schedule (fallback: the source's `firstPayDate`).
- `isExpenseLinkedToPaycheck` (payDates) decides it all, so Paychecks, hero "left over", month
  chart, pay date detail, moves/skips and reminders follow unchanged. Month schedules reuse
  `paycheckOrdinal` (default 1st paycheck). Every other paycheck counts paychecks from the anchor.
- Due days (HAU-349, merged into this branch 11-16): a due day now combines with the month
  schedules. `getExpenseRepeatMonths` treats a due-day bill as monthly unless it is 3/6/12 months;
  the due day picks the paycheck in each month it lands in. HAU-349's saved shape (dueDay, no
  schedule) still means monthly.
- `getMonthlyCost` (Expenses totals): every other = half of every paycheck, else amount / months.
- Form: How Often is a wrapping chip row shown for every linked source ("Monthly" hidden for a
  monthly source). With a due day typed it offers only Monthly / 3 / 6 months / Yearly and hides
  Which Paycheck. "Starting With" = next two paychecks; "First Month" = next 3, 6 or 12 months.
- Expenses rows: "15th in March", "1st paycheck in Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec", "Due the 18th in
  December"; cadence "yearly", "quarterly", "twice a year", "every other check". CSV "How
  Often" carries the schedule. Backup/restore needed nothing. 12 new strings in all 45 locales.
  Free (not labelled `Paid`).

## Pitfalls
