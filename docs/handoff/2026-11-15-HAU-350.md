# HAU-350: Bills that repeat every other paycheck, quarterly or yearly (2026-11-15)

## What changed
- `LinkSchedule` gains `every-other-paycheck`, `quarterly`, `semiannual`, `yearly`; `Expense`
  gains `repeatStart` (YYYY-MM-DD): the anchor pay date for every other paycheck, the 1st of a
  month it lands in for 3/6/12 months. Both repeat in both directions (past months in Pro history
  follow the same cycle). Old bills have no `repeatStart` and keep their schedule; a bill missing
  one falls back to the source's `firstPayDate`.
- `isExpenseLinkedToPaycheck` (payDates) decides it all, so Paychecks, the hero "left over", the
  month chart, the pay date detail, moves/skip markers and reminders follow without changes.
  Month-based schedules reuse `paycheckOrdinal` (Which Paycheck; default 1st), so "yearly in
  March" lands on March's 1st paycheck. Every other paycheck counts paychecks from the anchor
  with `getPaychecksThrough` (handles weekly/bi-weekly/semi-monthly/monthly and workday shifts).
- `getMonthlyCost`: every other = half of every paycheck, 3/6/12 months = amount / months. Used
  by the Expenses summary and section totals.
- Expense form: How Often is now a wrapping chip row (6 options, "Monthly" hidden for a monthly
  source where it equals every paycheck), shown for every linked source, not only 2+ paychecks a
  month. "Starting With" offers the next two paychecks (every other); "First Month" the next 3, 6
  or 12 months. A saved start in the past maps to the chip it repeats into.
- Expenses rows: meta "15th in March" / "1st paycheck in Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec", cadence "yearly",
  "quarterly", "twice a year", "every other check". CSV "How Often" column carries the schedule.
  Backup/restore needed nothing: the JSON holds the new fields.
- 12 new strings in all 45 locales. Free feature (issue not labelled `Paid`).
- `docs/AGENT_CONTEXT.md`: QA 27 simulator UDID corrected to `E48CA593-…` (the old one is gone).

## For QA
- Expenses > + > link a source > How Often chips. Free sees this month and next only, so use a
  first month of next month (or Pro for March). `expenses.yaml` taps nothing in How Often; the
  old segmented control is gone, so a flow that taps "Monthly" still works (chip text).

## Pitfalls
- Unlinked recurring bills have no schedule (assigned by hand); the repeat options only appear
  once a source is linked, as Monthly did before.
