# HAU-324: Past paychecks PRO row on top of main's segmented control (2026-11-14)

PR #68 (PRO controls at accessibility text sizes) conflicted with `main` in
`AppSegmentedControl.tsx` after HAU-162 merged its own stacking of that control. This branch is
that PR's work redone on current `main` (a rebase would have needed a force-push), and replaces
PR #68.

## What changed
- `app/history.tsx`: the "See earlier months" row wraps (`flexWrap: "wrap"` on the row,
  `flexShrink: 1` and centred text on the label). The label takes at most the row's width and
  breaks between words; the PRO badge and chevron drop to a centred second line when they do not
  fit beside it. Applies at every size, so a long translation wraps too.
- `AppSegmentedControl.tsx`: untouched, `main`'s version (HAU-162: stacked at accessibility
  sizes, single-line labels with `adjustsFontSizeToFit`). PR #68's wrapping variant is dropped:
  Add expense already passes with `main`'s.
- QA's flows from PR #68 carried over unchanged: `.maestro/qa/pro-text-size.yaml` (new) and the
  Past paychecks step in `smoke.yaml`.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` clean.
- `Paycheck Planner Dev` simulator (iOS 27), Release app with this branch's `export:embed`
  bundle, free tier, dark: `deep-link-approval` + `pro-text-size` + `smoke` pass. Screenshots
  read: Past paychecks at XL ("See earlier months" on one line, PRO and chevron under it) and
  XXXL (two lines, nothing cut at either edge); Add expense at XXXL (Bill over Set-aside,
  "Set-aside" whole, PRO inside the row). Both open the paywall.
- Not run: the rest of `.maestro/qa/`, light mode, RTL.

## Follow-ups / pitfalls
- PR #68 should be closed without merging once this one is in; its issue is fixed by this branch.
- Yoga only shrinks an item within its line: `flexWrap: "wrap"` plus `flexShrink: 1` on the text
  gives "wrap the label when it is alone on a line, otherwise move the badge down".
- A `Paycheck Planner Dev` simulator exists on this machine with a Release build. Copy its
  `.app`, write a fresh bundle into the copy with `bunx expo export:embed --platform ios --dev
  false --entry-file index.ts --bundle-output <copy>/main.jsbundle --assets-dest <copy>`, then
  `simctl install`: a JS-only change is verifiable in about a minute with no native build.
