  in `ship.config.json` (2 to 5 word titles, drafts for John). Bezel is `ship/iPhone 17 Pro - Deep Blue - Portrait.png`

  `simctl io recordVideo` and encodes `ship/previews/*.mp4`: 886x1920, H.264 High, 30 fps, silent stereo AAC,
  23.7 s and 26.6 s. Captions are the screenshot titles, faded in over the bottom of the clean recording.
- Store demo data only (`EXPO_PUBLIC_STORE_DEMO=1`): `buildStoreDemoData` in `src/utils/demoData.ts` adds bill
  icons and two set-asides with part-saved goals; `paycheck-planner://demo?short=1` adds a car repair that leaves

## Verified

  `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` clean.

## Pitfalls
- Captured on the dev build with a second Metro started with the flag (see the header of `ship/screenshots.yaml`).
- Maestro needs seconds per step, so a raw recording is mostly waiting. The preview script cuts every still stretch
  to 1.3 s (`freezedetect`). Its noise floor must stay tiny (0.00005): at 0.001 a check circle turning green counts
  as "still" and is cut. Caption times in `PREVIEWS` are tuned to that timeline; re-read the video after a re-record.
- On the dev build a check-off shows seconds after Maestro's tap (the flow waits for "Mark … unpaid"). Not checked
  on a Release build, so no ticket: it may only be Maestro's hierarchy reads holding the app.
- Dates follow the capture day. The month tap in `paychecks.yaml` works out next month's name itself.
