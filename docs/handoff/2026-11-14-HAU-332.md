# HAU-332: shorter "Mark all paid" in ml, kn, or, ta, hu (2026-11-14)

## What changed
- `payDate.markAllPaid` only, in five locale files. No code change.
  - ml "എല്ലാം അടച്ചതാക്കുക" (make all paid), or "ସବୁ ପଇଠ ମାର୍କ କରନ୍ତୁ" (mark all paid, the wording
    `autoMarkPaid` already uses).
  - kn "ಎಲ್ಲವನ್ನೂ ಗುರುತಿಸಿ", ta "அனைத்தையும் குறி", hu "Mind megjelölése": "mark all". No phrase
    with "paid" and a verb fits in these three; a bare "all paid" reads as a status, not a button.
- Why: the open pull request that caps the button at 40% of the bar shrinks the old 31 to 33
  character strings to its 0.7 floor (about 11 pt). The new ones fit the cap at or near full size.
- The row labels (`markPaid`, `markUnpaid`) and the toast (`markedAllPaid`) keep the long wording.

## Verified
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` and `bun run lint` clean.
- Widths measured with AppKit (system 17 pt semibold; the cap is about 161 pt on iPhone 17 Pro):
  old 254 to 285 pt, new hu 139, kn 136, ta 152, ml 158, or 159. For scale: de 204, te 191, th 227.
- `Paycheck Planner Dev` (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.0, dark, default text size): this branch's bundle
  with the 40% cap added by hand (not committed) copied over the Release build's
  `main.jsbundle`, `-AppleLanguages (<code>)`, sample data, first paycheck. Screenshots read for
  ml, kn, or, ta, hu, de: all five show the button whole and at the title's size (ml and or a
  touch under, both larger than German); the title is whole; hu, or and ta got Back's label back.
  Tapping the button marked every bill paid and the button left, in all five.
- Not checked: light mode, accessibility text sizes (the bar text is fixed at 1x), Android,
  narrower phones, a native speaker's read of the five strings, the `.maestro/qa/` suite.

## Follow-ups
- `.maestro/qa/pay-date-header-locale.yaml` exists only on the open 40%-cap branch, not on `main`,
  so it is not changed here. It finds the button by ml's old string in four steps; once both are
  merged it must use "എല്ലാം അടച്ചതാക്കുക" or the gate fails on that flow.
- Without the cap (today's `main`) the new strings simply take less of the bar than the old ones.
- th (227 pt) and te (191 pt) still shrink under the cap, th close to the floor.

## Lesson
- A string's rendered width, not its character count, decides the fit: th is 31 characters and
  227 pt, ta is 31 and 283 pt. Measure with `NSAttributedString.size()` in a Swift script.
