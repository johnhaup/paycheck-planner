# HAU-900 Paychecks: the header stays on screen through a month jump (2026-11-26)

## What changed and why
- Cause: HAU-867's tapped-jump fade set opacity on the whole FlatList, and the pinned header (hero,
  chart, pills) is part of it, so every pill/chart tap, Go to this month and short-paycheck line
  blanked the header for ~0.2 s. Nothing remounted; it was the fade.
- `app/(tabs)/(paychecks)/index.tsx`: the fade (`rowsFade`, `hidden ? 0 : jumpFade`) now goes on the
  rows and the footer only. The list's own opacity hides only the opening jump (`hidden` while
  `jumpFade` > 0; a tapped jump hides only once its rows are at 0), so launch is unchanged.
- `src/components/paychecks/PaycheckCell.tsx`: the cell is an `Animated.View` and takes the fade from
  the cell context (one animated style shared by every cell).
- A tapped jump from a collapsed header now brings the header out with `withTiming(0, TIMING.move)`
  at the tap instead of `collapse.set(0)` at landing, where the fade hid the pop. The chart's own
  bars/pills morph plays on the way, the same as scrolling back to the top.

## Verified (Runner 1, iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27.0, dev build on Metro, sample data, free)
- tsc and lint clean. `.maestro/qa/paychecks.yaml` and `smoke.yaml` pass.
- Recordings of Dec / Go to this month ×2 plus a collapsed-pill tap: header band (y 450-1050 px)
  pure background in 22 of 84 frames on main, 0 of 383 after. Dark and Light.
- Scratch React `Profiler` around the header (removed): 4 jumps, 102 `update` and 0 `mount`. The
  updates are the hero and meta line counting up. The hero counting from the old amount also shows
  it kept its state.

## Not verified
- Android (same JS; no large title), iPhone Duo, Reduce Motion (the collapse timing follows it; the
  fades do not, as before).

## Pitfalls
- The header still moves when a jump crosses the top of the list: the large title collapses or
  expands. That is UIKit's title, not a flash.
- A Profiler reporting by `fetch` missed the mount right after "Try with sample data"; a relaunch
  shows it. Check that a mount appears at all before reading "no mounts" as proof.
