# HAU-329: free chart keeps two months after the month's last paycheck (2026-11-14)

## What changed
- `src/utils/paycheckMonths.ts`, `buildMonthGroups` only. The window used to end at
  `endOfMonth(now + monthsAhead)`. It now ends `monthsAhead` months after the month of the **next
  paycheck** (earliest across sources, `getPaychecks(source, now, 1)`), falling back to `now`
  when there are no sources.
- Decision (product question in the issue): free always shows two months that have paychecks.
  While this month still has one, nothing changes (this month and next). After its last paycheck
  the chart is next month plus the one after, instead of next month alone. Pro gets the same
  rule and so always has twelve months (it had eleven on those days).
- A source whose first weekly/bi-weekly pay date is months away now shows its first two months
  instead of an empty window.
- `paydayReminders.ts` also calls `buildMonthGroups` (Pro default, from yesterday); only the far
  end of its year moves, by at most a month. No UI, string or flow changes.

  below. The suite on today's date, Pro in the app and Android were not run.

## Follow-ups
- `paychecks.yaml` and `month-chart.yaml` fail on any day after the month's last paycheck, with
  or without this change: both look for a column that is "(this month)", and the calendar month
  is not in the chart then. `paychecks.yaml` taps the first column not labelled "(this month)",
  which is the selected one. The gate will fail on such real days (sample data: after the last
  Friday paycheck of a month) until the flows stop assuming it.
- The open continuous-list PR (HAU-316) edits the same function and adds this month's past
  paychecks for free. Merged with this, free shows this month (past only), next and the one
  after on those days; decide there whether that is wanted. The `until` lines will conflict.
