## Description (4000 max)

Paycheck Planner budgets the way you actually get paid: one paycheck at a time.

Add your income sources and bills, and every upcoming paycheck shows which bills it covers and how much is left once they're paid.

PLAN EVERY PAYCHECK
• See each upcoming paycheck with its bills and what's left
• Browse month by month with a chart of what's left after bills
• Check bills off as you pay them and track progress per paycheck

FITS YOUR PAY SCHEDULE
• Weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly (like the 15th and 30th), or monthly
• For twice-a-month pay, shift weekend paydays to the Friday before
• Track more than one income source

BILLS THAT LAND IN THE RIGHT PLACE
• Link a recurring bill to an income source so it's added automatically
• Choose whether it comes out of every paycheck or just one paycheck a month
• Add one-time expenses to a specific paycheck, and move them if plans change
• Bill amount different this month? Change it for just that paycheck

PAY IN ONE TAP
• Save a payment link with each bill and open it right from your paycheck
• Optionally mark a bill paid when you tap its link

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• No account, no sign-up, no bank connection
• Nothing is collected or shared; everything is stored on your device

Light and dark mode, plus an option to show whole-dollar amounts.
